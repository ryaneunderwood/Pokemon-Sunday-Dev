[x] v21 was still running Essentials' 311 stock sample animations. v17 had 1250

SPRITE METRICS — REPAIRED (tools/fix_sprite_metrics.py)
[x] 901 BackSprite entries had y = 0. That was right for v21's original
      bottom-aligned back sprites, but the Gen 9 Pack import overwrote 1644 of
      them with 288x288 art that centres the Pokemon and leaves 27-99px of empty

      Pokemon's back sprite was rendering that far above the platform.
